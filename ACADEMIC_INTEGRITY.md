# Academic Integrity and Attribution

Your submission must represent your own understanding. You may discuss concepts and debugging strategies with classmates, but you must write and explain your own implementation.

If the course permits generative-AI tools, add an **AI Use Declaration** to `report.md` that lists:

1. the tool used;
2. the prompts or task types for which it was used;
3. the files or functions affected; and
4. how you verified and understood the resulting code.

You must cite third-party code, models, images, and data. During a short code review, you may be asked to explain any submitted function or experiment. Code that cannot be explained may receive no credit for the affected criterion.
